About

I'm Portfolio Manager of RM Funds. We want to start a partnerships modeled after the original Buffett partnerships. The fund will have no management fee, a high water mark, and only charges a performance fee on profits that exceed a 6% hurdle. We view this website as a vehicle that allows us to keep track of our portfolio performance. As of now, there are no outside investors.

My investing journey began during the COVID-19 market crash in 2020. When the lockdowns were announced and I believed that the pandemic was a temporary disruption and that human adaptability would eventually bring life back to normal. I tried pursuing my father to invest in stock that could endure the crisis, but my father was not interested.

Instead, my dad gave a small capital to invest on my own. This led me to begin reading and learning about businesses, markets, and capital allocation. Eventually, he discovered his investing philosophy through books such as The Intelligent Investor. My first stocks were ITC and Tata Motors. At the time, however, I had no understanding of stock markets and invested based on the belief that these stocks that had fallen significantly in price would eventually recover
